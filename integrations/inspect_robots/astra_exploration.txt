Task: Execute one pick-and-place episode on YAM3 using ONLY the logical right arm (`right_j0`..`right_j5`, `right_gripper`) to pick up the pink block, place it centered on the black X on the white paper, and return the arm to its initial resting pose before calling `done`. Keep the left arm strictly at its initial pose. Never trigger a blind hardware reset/park command.

Control & Camera Setup:
- Tool: `move_joints` with absolute right-arm targets in radians (`right_gripper`: `1.0` = open, `0.0` = closed).
- Per-call limits: up to `0.80 rad` delta per arm joint (`right_j0`..`right_j5`) in clear space, and up to `1.0` delta on `right_gripper` (full open/close in one call).
- Views: `main` (angled table view for overall clearance and placement verification) and `right` (wrist view for approach depth relative to the finger pads and XY centering).

YAM3 Right-Arm Kinematic Priors:
- `right_j0` (Base yaw): `+` sweeps inward/left across the table; `-` sweeps outward/right.
- `right_j1` (Shoulder) + `right_j2` (Elbow): Increasing both together (`+j1`, `+j2` at ~1:1) extends reach radially outward at roughly constant height. Increasing `+j1` relative to `j2` lowers the fingertips toward the table; decreasing `-j1` relative to `j2` lifts them.
- `right_j3` (Wrist pitch): `-` pitches the gripper and wrist camera downward (`j3 ≈ -0.60` points vertically down).
- `right_j4` & `right_j5` (Wrist roll/flange): Keep near initial neutral angles (`~ -0.03`, `~ -0.07`) so the jaw crossbar stays level with the table. Only rotate `j5` if the block is visibly skewed by >25° relative to the table grid.

Execution Policy:
1. Coupled Moves: Combine active joints (`j0`..`j3`) simultaneously (`0.40–0.80 rad` per joint in clear transit; `0.10–0.20 rad` during descent; `0.02–0.05 rad` only during final XY centering right above the target).
2. Wrist-Camera Depth Check Before Closing:
   - Do not close the gripper based on overhead alignment alone. Use `right` as the primary approach-depth view: a small, distant block centered between the fingers is not yet at grasp depth. Compare successive wrist views as you descend; the block must occupy the central grasp region between the pads, with both pads visibly alongside opposite sides rather than hovering above its top or catching one corner.
   - Do NOT close `right_gripper` until BOTH depth and XY alignment are supported by this pad-relative geometry in `right`. Apparent image size is supporting evidence, not a fixed frame-fill threshold or a calibrated distance: a tipped block can also look large. Use `main` to cross-check table/arm clearance; apparent overlap in `main` must never override an unresolved wrist-view gap. If the pad-to-side relationship is obscured, re-observe or reposition with clearance before closing.
3. Grasp, Lift & Miss Recovery:
   - Command `right_gripper: 0.0` in one call. A candidate grasp stalls `right_gripper > 0.10` and holds steady when lifted `~6–8 cm` (`-j1`, `+j2`); confirm visually that the block actually rises with the pads before lateral transfer. Closure readback alone is not proof of a grasp.
   - If `right_gripper` closes to `< 0.10`, treat the grasp as missed, not proof that height was the only error. Reopen (`1.0`) if the opening path is clear, then reassess wrist depth and lateral/fore-aft centering. Only when the jaws are visibly above an upright block with clear descent, descend decisively (`+0.12` to `+0.18 rad` on `right_j1`), re-observe, and satisfy Rule 2 before retrying. If the block shifts, tips, or catches a pad, use contact recovery first; do not keep descending along the same misaligned path.
4. Table-Contact Guard & Centered Release on X:
   - After every move, compare `measured` vs commanded `targets`. Treat `> 0.015 rad` lag on `j1`/`j2`, unexpected wrist deflection, or visible block tipping as possible contact: stop further downward motion and reassess. A small residual alone does not identify the contact location. Stopping descent does not mean ending the episode.
   - For recoverable block/table contact, keep the current gripper opening and withdraw slightly upward (`-j1`, with `+j2` only if needed), or reverse the last descent toward a previously clear pose, only when the withdrawal path is supported by the views and feedback and all hardware guards permit it. Re-observe after that bounded move; confirm separation and easing residuals before re-centering above the block, then descend with Rule 2. Do not sweep sideways, rotate the wrist, or force the gripper while a pad is caught. If withdrawal drags the block, increases deflection, or is blocked, stop and reassess rather than forcing recovery.
   - Translate over the white paper until `right` shows the held block centered directly over the black `X` mark. Lower until `main` shows the bottom of the block touching or within ~1–2 cm of the paper, then immediately open `right_gripper: 1.0` in one call. Never press the block into the table.
5. Verify, Return to Rest & Finish:
   - Retract upward `~10 cm` (`-j1`, `+j2`) and verify in `main` that the pink block rests inside the white paper on the X.
   - Return all right-arm joints (`right_j0`..`right_j5`, `right_gripper: 1.0`) back to their exact step-0 initial readback angles in 2 coupled `move_joints` calls (`≤ 0.80 rad/joint`), then call `done`.

Guards & Notes:
- Nudging the foam block is recoverable—NEVER call `give_up` because the block shifted on the table. Only call `give_up` on hardware/CAN faults, persistent safety-guard blocks with no permitted recovery direction, physical unreachability, or when no clear withdrawal/repositioning path can be established. For contact-related `give_up`, state why upward withdrawal or reversal is unavailable; contact alone is not a terminal condition. Never bypass a hardware guard.
