Task
Use only YAM3's logical right arm (`right_j0`..`right_j5`, `right_gripper`) to pick up the loose USB-A plug and fully seat its metal tip into any of the four top-facing hub ports. Release and withdraw clear. Keep the left arm at its initial pose.

Controls & Views
- `move_joints` takes absolute targets in radians (`0.80 rad/joint` max delta; `1.0` max gripper delta).
- Keep `right_gripper` in `0.0` (closed) to `0.90` (open). Never command `> 0.90` or `right_j3 < -1.65`.
- Views: `main` (check if both black fingertips are at the same height above the mat, and check clearance) and `right` (XY centering and `j5` alignment).

YAM3 Right-Arm Geometry
- `right_j0`: `+` sweeps inward/left; `-` sweeps outward/right.
- `right_j1` + `right_j2`: Increasing both together extends reach forward. Keep `right_j1 > right_j2` (high elbow) so `right_j3` has room to point straight down without hitting `-1.65 rad`.
- `right_j3`: More negative (`-`) pitches the fingertips downward toward the base; less negative (`+`) tilts them forward toward the hub.
- `right_j4`: Keep at `0.0`.
- `right_j5`: Rotates the jaws around the vertical axis to flank the plug's narrow sides (or align with a hub port).

Execution
1. **Deploy & Level Fingertips Above Plug (3–4 calls):**
   - Open `right_gripper: 0.90`. Extend above the plug housing (`j1 > j2`, `j4 = 0.0`, `j3 ≈ -1.35`).
   - Center the black housing between the open jaws in `right` view and rotate `j5` so the pads flank the housing's long sides with the metal tip exposed outside the pads.
   - **Visual Level Check in `main`:** Look at the two black fingertips in `main`. If the front/right fingertip hangs lower than the back/left fingertip, pitch `right_j3` more negative (`-0.15` to `-0.25 rad`) until both fingertips sit at the exact same height parallel to the mat.
2. **Snug-to-Table Grasp (2–3 calls):**
   - Lower straight down (`+j1`, small `-j2`) until `main` shows both fingertips touching the mat OR `measured` lags `targets` by `> 0.015 rad`.
   - **As soon as the fingertips touch the mat with the housing centered in `right`, STOP lowering and immediately close `right_gripper: 0.0` in the very next call—do NOT retreat before closing.**
   - Lift `~6 cm` (`-j1`). If the plug lifts with the metal tip exposed, proceed to Step 3. If it missed, reopen to `0.90`, adjust XY, lower until snug on the mat again, and close.
3. **Align Over Hub & Insert:**
   - Move `~5 cm` above the hub. Verify in `main` that the metal tip points straight down (`90°` to the hub top) and align `j5` with a port slot in `right` view.
   - Center over the chosen port and lower in small steps (`0.02–0.04 rad` on `j1`). Stop on hub shift, plug bind, or `> 0.015 rad` lag. Once seated, open `right_gripper: 0.90`, lift clear, verify it stays seated, and call `done`.
