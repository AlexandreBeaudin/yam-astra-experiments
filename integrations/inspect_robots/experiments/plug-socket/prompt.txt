Task
Use only YAM3's right arm (right_j0..right_j5, right_gripper) to pick up the charger brick and horizontally insert its two flat prongs into any valid vertical 2-prong slot pair on the upright white power strip. Release and withdraw clear; keep the left arm parked.
Mechanical insertion setup: the power strip is unplugged and mounted on its side so the socket face is vertical (perpendicular to the mat) and braced from behind.

Controls & Materials
- move_joints uses absolute radians; max change 0.80 rad/joint and 1.0 gripper per call. All interpolation, bounds, and hardware guards apply.
- right_gripper: 0.0 closed, 0.90 open; NEVER command >0.90 or right_j3 < -1.65. Keep right_j4 within [-1.35, 1.35] rad and a high elbow (right_j1 > right_j2).
- The charger brick has a white body, dark perimeter rim, USB-C port on the rear face, and two parallel flat metal prongs (~1.7 cm long) projecting from the front face.
- Pinch the REAR HALF of the brick so the front half and BOTH prongs stick out cleanly past the black pads.
- The upright white power strip has four stations in a row (left to right, with a power button on the far right). Each station has two vertical parallel 2-prong slot pairs (an upper pair and a middle pair) above angled 3-prong slots.
- Choose ANY ONE of the eight vertical parallel 2-prong slot pairs; avoid the angled 3-prong slots at the bottom.

Views & Geometry
- Only main and right are active. left is parked and MASKED; report left visible=false, regions=[].
- main: side/oblique view for fingertip-to-mat contact, brick pitch levelness (Z), workspace clearance, and flush seating depth against the vertical socket face.
- right: wrist camera looking forward along the gripper/prongs for XY alignment, squareness to the socket face, and prong-to-slot keying.
- j0 positive sweeps inward/left, negative outward/right. Increasing j1+j2 extends horizontal reach forward; coordinate small j1/j2/j3 adjustments to keep the brick level and move straight horizontally into the socket.
- Yaw geometry (critical): the brick starts pointing away from the rail (0 deg), while the vertical socket face faces right (90 deg / ~1.57 rad left of the brick's initial heading). Total horizontal turn `(right_j0 - pickup_j0) + abs(right_j4)` must reach ~1.57 rad so the prongs enter at 90 degrees—this typically requires right_j4 around -1.05 to -1.30 rad (approaching a middle station around right_j0 = +0.30 to +0.45 rad reduces required wrist yaw).
- Keep right_j5 on the negative side (~ -1.05 to -1.65 rad) during alignment so the wrist camera stays on the prong side and both flat prongs are vertical (| |) to match the vertical slots.

Execution
1. Snug-to-Table Grasp
- Open 0.90; deploy above the rear half of the brick with both prongs exposed ahead of the pads in right.
- Do NOT close early based on right-view overlap alone (the brick looks centered even 5 cm above the mat). Lower until main view confirms the black pad tips touch/graze the mat surface OR measured arm lag exceeds 0.015 rad.
- Stop lowering and immediately close right_gripper=0.0 in the NEXT call (a valid brick grasp reads back ~0.20–0.52; <0.03 means empty). Do NOT retreat before closing. If empty, lift clear (-j1) and open 0.90 BEFORE lowering again—never open and descend in the same call.
- Lift a few centimetres (-j1) so the brick clears the mat, and verify both prongs remain cleanly exposed.

2. Square & Level Alignment Over Target Slot Pair
- If both prongs are already vertical and clearly exposed ahead of the pads, proceed directly to alignment rather than setting the brick down, even if it rests on its narrow edge.
- Sweep and extend in clear space in front of the vertical socket face (aiming near a middle station around right_j0 = +0.30 to +0.45 rad), turning right_j4 (~ -1.05 to -1.30 rad) and rolling right_j5 (~ -1.05 to -1.65 rad) until:
  (a) in right view, the white power strip face runs straight/horizontal across the image (NOT receding diagonally at an angle) and both vertical prong tips line up with your chosen vertical 2-prong slot pair; and
  (b) in main view, the brick body and prongs are horizontal (0 deg level with the mat) and the brick front face is parallel to the vertical socket wall.

3. Guarded Horizontal Insertion & Release
- Hard gate before pushing: do NOT advance into the socket unless the strip face is squared across right view and the brick is level in main view.
- Advance horizontally in small observed steps (0.02–0.04 rad in j1/j2, adjusting j0/j3 slightly to hold height and lateral centering), verifying true entry by decreasing exposed metal prong length in right view and closing brick-to-strip gap in main view.
- Stop immediately if the brick tips in the pads without prong shortening, the strip shifts, or arm tracking lag exceeds 0.015 rad; back out slightly (-j1, -j2) to re-square before retrying. Never push through a stop.
- Once the brick front face is seated flush against the vertical strip face, open right_gripper=0.90, withdraw straight back (-j1, -j2) clear of the brick, verify it stays seated in main/right, and call done. If no supported alignment remains, explain it in give_up.
