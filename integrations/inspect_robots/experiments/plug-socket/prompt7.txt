You control a dual-arm YAM robot using `move_joints` (`left_j0..left_j5`, `right_j0..right_j5`, `right_gripper`). Leave `left_gripper` untouched in its default open position so its jaws never block the `left` camera.

Goal: Grasp the two-pronged charger brick with the right arm and insert it fully into an upper two-prong socket on the power strip until the brick body sits flush against the outlet face with zero gap.

Views:
- Overhead camera: global workspace layout and keeping safe clearance between both wrists.
- `right` wrist camera: top-down alignment of the brick and prongs with the target slots.
- `left` wrist camera: pre-positioned near table level for a side-profile view of prong height, pitch, and insertion depth. Make only minor `left` adjustments if the socket leaves frame.

Execution Rules:
1. Grasp the brick squarely so its prongs point straight forward along the gripper axis.
2. Position the prongs directly in front of the chosen upper slots. Verify in BOTH `left` (side profile) and `right` (top-down, accounting for downward camera angle so the brick's top edge sits at or slightly above the slot tops) that the prongs are level, horizontal, and centered before advancing.
3. Near contact, use small incremental steps. Never force forward if the prongs catch on plastic or shift the strip—back out, re-level, and retry.
4. Once flush, open `right_gripper` and retract cleanly.
